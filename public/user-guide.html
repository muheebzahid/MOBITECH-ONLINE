Section 03
🏷️
Deals — Managing Auction Purchases
Every batch of phones bought at auction is a "Deal." The Deals module tracks each deal from auction win to final sale — all dates, costs, funding, and status changes.

Creating a New Deal

1

Click "+ New Deal"

Find this green button in the top right corner of the Deals page.

2

Fill in Deal Information

Enter the Vendor (ATT, EcoATM, Other), Auction Platform, Date, and phone details — Model, Storage, Grade, Color, Carrier.

3

Enter Financials

Set Unit Cost (USD), Quantity, and Funding Source (Amex, Cash Pool, Mixed). Totals calculate automatically.

4

Save the Deal

Click "Create Deal". A deal number is auto-generated (e.g., ATT-2026-0001) and status is set to Auction Won.

Deal Status Lifecycle

PAYMENT_REQUIRED

Fully sold and settled
✅
Bulk Status Update

Select multiple deals using checkboxes, then click "Advance Selected" to move all of them to the next status at once — perfect when a shipment of multiple lots arrives together.

Section 04
📥
Bulk Upload — Importing Deals via CSV
Import many deals at once from Excel/CSV. This is the fastest way to enter data into the system.
1

Download the Template

On the Deals page, click the grey "Download Template" button. This downloads a CSV with correct columns and an example row.

2

Fill in Your Deals in Excel

Open in Excel or Google Sheets. Each row = one deal. Delete the example row and add your real data.

3

Save as CSV

In Excel: File → Save As → select "CSV (Comma delimited)" format.

4

Upload the File

Click the orange "Upload Bulk Deals" button, select your CSV, and wait for the confirmation message.

💾
Automatic Backup After Every Upload

After every successful bulk upload, the system saves a complete backup of all your deals to your laptop. This backup is automatically restored if the database ever resets — your data is always safe.

Section 05
📱
Inventory — Stock Overview
The Inventory page shows all phones available for sale — units per model, cost price, and remaining quantity after invoicing.
🔍
Search & Filter
Search by model, filter by status, or sort any column to find what you need instantly.
📊
Remaining Quantity
Each row shows available units — original quantity minus everything already invoiced.
💵
Landed Cost
See your cost per unit (purchase + fees) so you always know your minimum selling price.
🚢
Location Status
Know exactly where your stock is — in USA, in transit, or at your Dubai warehouse.
ℹ️
Inventory is Automatic

No manual updates needed. When you create a deal, units appear in inventory. When you raise a sales invoice, those units are automatically deducted from stock.

Section 06
🚢
Logistics — Shipment Tracking
Track shipments from the USA to Dubai. Multiple deals can be grouped into a single shipment (container or courier).
1

Create a New Shipment

Click "+ New Shipment" and give it a name (e.g., "Container July 2026").

2

Assign Deals

Select which deals are being shipped together. They must be in AT_TURBO_LOGISTICS status or later.

3

Track Dates

Update Shipped USA Date, Arrived Dubai Date, and Received by Mobitech Date as the shipment progresses.

4

Log Turbo Invoice

Enter the shipping invoice amount from Turbo Logistics and mark it paid once settled.

Section 07
🧾
Sales Invoices — Billing Your Customers
When you sell phones to customers, raise a Sales Invoice linked to specific deals. Specifies which units are sold and at what price.
1

Click "+ New Invoice"

Go to the Sales Invoices page and click to create a new invoice.

2

Select Customer

Choose from your company list (SB Technology, Mobitech Wireless, etc.) or enter a new customer name.

3

Add Line Items

For each line, select the Deal, enter Model, Quantity, and Unit Price (AED). Add as many lines as needed.

4

Issue & Download PDF

Click "Create Invoice". The invoice gets an auto number (e.g., INV-2026-0001). Download it as PDF to send to your customer.

💡
Profit Calculation

Once an invoice is issued, the system automatically calculates Gross Profit based on the selling price vs. your landed cost. View this on the Deal Detail page.

Section 09
🤝
Partners — Equity & Profit Sharing
Manages the three business partners — tracking their equity shares, capital contributions, withdrawals, and profit distributions.
💼
Capital Injections
Record when a partner puts money into the business. Increases their account balance.
💰
Profit Shares
Distribute profits by equity percentage. Each distribution is logged with date and amount.
🏧
Withdrawals
Record when a partner withdraws money from their account.
📋
Transaction History
Full ledger for each partner — fully auditable and date-stamped.
ℹ️
Default Equity Split

Muheeb 33.34% · Beshair 33.33% · Faisal 33.33%. Contact the Super Admin to update these percentages if they change.

Section 10
💳
Treasury — Amex & Cash Pool
Tracks your two funding sources showing available capacity, deployed funds, and repayment history.
💳
Amex Credit Limit
Shows your total Amex limit and how much is tied up in open deals. Alerts you when approaching your limit.
🏦
Cash Pool Balance
Shows total cash pool size and deployed cash. Tracks the 7% p.a. finance cost on deployed cash days.
📤
Wire Transfers
Log outgoing wire transfers to auction houses. Each wire is linked to the relevant deal.
📥
Repayments
Record Amex payments or cash replenishments to free up capacity for future deals.
⚙️
Updating Credit Limits

If your Amex limit or Cash Pool size changes, go to Treasury, click the gear icon ⚙️ on the card, and update the limit. Changes take effect immediately.
